I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the [Geometric Machine Learning Group](https://weber.seas.harvard.edu){:target="_blank"} at Harvard University, where I work with [Melanie Weber](https://melanie-weber.com){:target="_blank"}. I recently received my PhD in [Statistics](https://stat.uchicago.edu){:target="_blank"} from the University of Chicago, where I was advised by [Victor Veitch](http://victorveitch.com/){:target="_blank"}.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I received a B.S. in Statistics and Mathematics from [Seoul National University](https://stat.snu.ac.kr/en/){:target="_blank"}.

My research focuses on developing theoretical foundations for AI systems, with an emphasis on understanding the geometric and causal structure of representations of large language models.
